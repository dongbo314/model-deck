# Model licenses

Model Deck Core does not contain or automatically download model weights.

Users are responsible for obtaining models or provider access under the applicable upstream terms. Future capability packs must record, for every model:

- exact model name and revision;
- upstream URL;
- license identifier and full text;
- redistribution and commercial-use permissions;
- artifact checksum; and
- whether the model is bundled, downloaded by an installer, or supplied by the user.

No model may be added to a release until this information is complete.
